\# Current Objective



The current objective is to build the official website of Zenova Enterprises.



The website should establish trust, communicate professionalism and represent the company as an enterprise technology brand rather than a freelance portfolio or generic marketing agency.



Version One focuses entirely on creating a strong first impression.



Future versions will introduce additional products, pages and services.

\# Decision Record



The company intentionally studies established enterprise technology companies to understand the reasoning behind excellent design decisions.



Infosys currently serves as the primary reference for interaction patterns, navigation behavior and layout structure.



The objective is to learn from proven design systems rather than imitate visual identity.



Zenova should continuously evolve toward its own recognizable design language as the company matures.
