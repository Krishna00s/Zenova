\# Design References



These images represent the visual direction of Zenova.



They are not templates.



They are not layouts to recreate.



They are a collection of ideas that define how Zenova should feel.



Study them carefully before designing anything.



Pay attention to the atmosphere, the pacing, the typography, the whitespace and the overall rhythm.



Our goal is not to reproduce these references.



Our goal is to understand why they work.



Then build something that feels unmistakably like Zenova.



\---



\# Reference One



This reference establishes the foundation of the homepage.



Focus on



\- overall atmosphere

\- visual hierarchy

\- editorial typography

\- card composition

\- spacing

\- colour relationships

\- page rhythm



It represents the first impression visitors should have when they arrive.



Calm.



Thoughtful.



Welcoming.



\---



\# Reference Two



This reference continues the story.



Study how trust is created through layout rather than decoration.



Observe



\- editorial composition

\- asymmetry

\- photography

\- whitespace

\- reading rhythm

\- section transitions

\- visual balance



The objective is to create confidence without becoming loud.



\---



\# Photography



Photography should always feel authentic.



Avoid staged business imagery.



Prefer moments that feel real.



People working.



Sketching.



Reviewing.



Building.



Creating.



Natural light is preferred.



Real moments build trust faster than perfect renders.



\---



\# Motion



The references show a visual direction.



They do not define animation.



Motion should support storytelling.



Scrolling should feel continuous.



Sections should transition naturally.



Animations should remain subtle.



Nothing should move simply because it can.



Movement should always have a reason.



\---



\# Originality



Never copy these references.



Instead, learn from them.



Understand their spacing.



Their typography.



Their proportions.



Their restraint.



Their emotional tone.



Every final design should feel like it belongs to Zenova.



Not to the reference images.



\---



\# Final Reminder



Before creating anything, ask yourself:



Does this feel calm?



Does this feel thoughtful?



Does this feel human?



Does this feel handcrafted?



If the answer is yes,



you're moving in the right direction.
